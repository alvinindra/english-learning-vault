# Start Here - Sistem Belajar

**Goal:** Speak and write technical English confidently while working as a developer.

## Daily Loop (15-25 min)
1. **Input (5 min):** Open one vocabulary note in [[01 Vocabulary]]. Read aloud.
2. **Practice (10 min):** Go to [[02 Phrases/Daily Standup]] and shadow 3 sentences.
3. **Recall (5-10 min):** Review [[04 Practice/Flashcards]] with Spaced Repetition plugin.

## Structure
- [[01 Vocabulary]]: words by domain
- [[02 Phrases]]: sentences you actually say at work
- [[03 Grammar]]: patterns for docs and commands
- [[04 Practice]]: active recall

## Tips
- Link everything: `[[function]]` connects English to Indonesian example.
- Use tags: #frontend #backend #meeting
- Keep examples real: copy from your PRs, tickets, or standups.
